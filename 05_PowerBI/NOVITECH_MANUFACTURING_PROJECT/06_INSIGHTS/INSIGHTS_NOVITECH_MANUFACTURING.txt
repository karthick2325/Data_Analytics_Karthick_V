•	Production is concentrated in Munich Electronics and Tokyo Assembly, while the remaining plants contribute lower production volumes.
•	Energy efficiency differs substantially across plants; Shanghai Battery Gigafactory and Detroit Engine Plant show much higher displayed energy consumption per unit than Munich Electronics and Tokyo Assembly.
•	Procurement spending is concentrated in Apex Lithium Corp and Lithium-Ion Cells, creating a visible cost concentration.
•	Several suppliers show high delay rates, particularly Global Packaging Logistics and MexiStamping S.A.
•	Material dependency is concentrated in shared materials, especially Copper Wire Spool, Electrical Connectors, High-Strength Steel Sheets, and Microcontrollers.
•	Defect volume, downtime, and energy intensity should be reviewed together rather than using production volume alone as an efficiency indicator.
