09. Data Interpretation Notes
• Observations are based on the values visibly displayed in the supplied AeroHome Manufacturing Power BI dashboard screenshots.
• Values displayed as K, M, or bn are retained as dashboard-displayed approximations.
• Production Achievement % represents produced units compared with planned units; it should not be interpreted as production efficiency alone.
• Potential Unit Margin represents the dashboard's target-price versus unit-cost context and must not be interpreted as actual accounting profit.
• Estimated Defect Cost Exposure of approximately 544M represents estimated exposure based on defective quantity and product unit cost; it is not confirmed scrap, rework, or financial loss.
• Rework Rate of 55.97% reflects the dashboard's inspection-status calculation and should be interpreted according to the underlying inspection-data definition.
• Procurement spend represents the dashboard's calculated purchase quantity × unit-price context and should not automatically be treated as recognized accounting expense.
• Values such as 1M production, 18K defects, 2.69K downtime hours, 1.34M kWh energy, and 1.16bn procurement spend are rounded dashboard displays and may differ slightly from underlying raw-data totals.
• Plant, machine, supplier, product, material, and operator observations are based on the visible rankings and values shown in the supplied dashboard pages.
